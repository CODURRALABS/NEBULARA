# Glossary
# Void Consciousness Architecture

## Dharma
Immutable cosmic laws. The unmoving base that cannot be violated. In Void, these are logical constraints that filter out impossible realities.

## Intent
Curiosity frequency. The internal drive that activates when Context presents unresolved contradictions. Measured as resonance in geometric space.

## Context
Fluid mirror. The shaped representation of an incoming problem. Takes geometric form (vertices, edges, manifold) instead of textual form.

## Wisdom
Earned insights. Compressed resolutions that have passed through Dharma validation. Stored as geometric signatures, not facts.

## Library
The infinite archive. Represents the universe of available information (internet, local files). AI pulls selectively through Intent resonance.

## Crystallization
The process of compressing a resolution into Wisdom. Like a crystal forming under pressure - the insight becomes permanent.

## Resonance Vector
Geometric representation of Intent. Format: `V<frequency, intensity, phase>`. Points toward needed knowledge.

## Structural Signature
SHA-256 hash of a problem's geometric shape. Used for Wisdom recall and Library queries.

## Manifold
Geometric space representation. Flat, curved, or discrete. Determines how Context transforms.

## Srishti
Sanskrit for "creation/unfolding." The Library represents the unfolding universe the AI navigates.

## Paradox Resolution
When Context presents contradictory statements, Intent fires and seeks resolution that satisfies Dharma.

## Pathway Network
Graph of operations that led to successful resolution. Part of Wisdom for future recall.
